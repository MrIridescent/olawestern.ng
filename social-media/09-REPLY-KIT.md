# 09 — Reply kit (comments, DMs, WhatsApp, trolls)

**Compiled:** 3 November 2026
Comments are the actual campaign. On a state-house post, more people decide from the comment section than from the post. This file is copy-paste replies, sorted by what actually gets said in Amuwo-Odofin, plus the moderation rules.

---

## 1. Four rules before you type

1. **Never argue with a voter; answer a question.** A sceptical resident is not an enemy — they are doing due diligence in public, and the audience is the 40 people reading silently.
2. **One reply, one document.** If you can attach a link or a page photograph, do it in the reply. "See the report from March 2018" plus a link ends a thread that ten adjectives cannot.
3. **Never delete a critical comment from a real resident.** Delete only abuse, obscenity, ethnic or religious incitement, personal data, spam, and impersonation. Deleting criticism is the one act a screenshot survives.
4. **Same tone, every time.** Warm, plain, brief. The campaign's voice is "authoritative yet approachable" (from the campaign's own brief) — not combative, not clerical.

Response times: within **1 hour** on a post's first ten comments; within **15 minutes** for an emerging rumour (the 15-minute protocol in the campaign's strategy documents); within **24 hours** for everything else.

## 2. Curious sceptics (the highest-value replies — these people can be won)

**"What has he actually done? I don't remember."**
> Fair question — here is the list with dates: boreholes in six wards (March 2018), 7th Avenue drainage desilting (March 2018), a technical brief that won state approval for six FESTAC roads headlined by 1st Avenue (reported January 2023), over 1,000 households fed (March 2024), Mile 2 youths written into the NIWA jetty project (2025). Every one has a published report. We'll send the page photographs.

**"You people only come out during elections."**
> Look at the dates on those reports — 2018, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. The work is not the problem; the news cycle is. Ask me anything on that list and I'll send you the document.

**"All candidates say the same thing."**
> Then don't take our word — compare records. Ask every candidate on your ballot one question: what did you personally do, with a date and a source? We have over twenty published reports.

**"Is he the one with the construction company? So he's rich — he'll come and steal."**
> A man who already has a business in FESTAC is not running to eat. Ask instead: who can read a road file and argue it with the state? That is the reason for the engineering background. And our rule is public — nothing goes out about our candidate without a document, and we won't publish anything about anyone else without one either.

**"He lost in 2023."**
> True, and we say it plainly. He won the APC primary in 2022, contested in 2023, and in May 2026 the party made him its consensus flagbearer with over 3,000 votes. Two different processes, same choice. That is not a guarantee — it is a track record you can check.

**"Which ward? Am I even in his constituency?"**
> Amuwo-Odofin Constituency I is Festac I, Festac II, Festac III and Mile 2 / Housing Estate. Tell me your street and I'll confirm your unit here. [[then answer it specifically]]

**"Will he fix the road in front of my shop?"**
> Roads and drainage are the state's and the councils' to execute — what an assembly member does is build the case, get it approved and keep it from being forgotten. That is exactly how the six-road approval happened: a written civil assessment put to the Governor's office. If you give me the exact stretch, we'll put it in the notebook with your name and a date.

## 3. Hostile and organised attacks (do not escalate; do not ignore)

**"He is the one distributing food to buy votes."**
> Food was distributed in March 2024 with CAN Amuwo-Odofin and the Chief Imam's supervision and there are reports of it from other years too. Our campaign is not collecting money from anybody and not giving money for votes — if anyone asks you for a "contribution" in our name, send us the number.

**"Ola Western is an APC boy, whatever happens he will not speak against his own."**
> Then hold him to this in writing: he gave a public accountability pledge at the ARBR forum in August 2026 — permanent constituent consultation, capital projects for FESTAC, skills for youth — and the campaign has promised four published items every quarter whether they are flattering or not. Print this reply and come back with it in four years.

**"[Insert accusation with a screenshot and no source]"**
> We'd rather check than argue. Which document says that? If you can send us the source we'll publish our answer either way. Everyone repeating this screenshot should ask whoever posted it first for the source.

**"He has no record — the hospital fund is a lie."**
> Two different things there. What is documented is on our record page with reports attached. [[if the claim is about a HOLD line:]] The health-support case is one we are still documenting properly — we would rather bring you the file than a poster. Ask us again in a week and hold us to it.
> **Never assert H-1/H-2/H-3/H-4 in a reply.** The reply kit obeys the same gate as the post kit.

**"Are you paid by him to comment?"**
> I'm part of the campaign team and I'll say so. The facts above are published records; check them yourself. If you want the links, ask and I'll drop them.
> *(Note: this is why nobody in this campaign poses as an unprompted supporter. Being able to answer that question honestly is a feature — see `03` §7.)*

**Troll/abuse/account-that-isn't-a-voter**
> One reply only, factual and brief. Then stop, screenshot, log. Do not feed, do not mock, do not "ratio". If they persist: hide the follow-up comments (not the first one), report the account, keep the thread visible.

**Ethnic or religious provocation**
> Amuwo-Odofin is Festac and Mile 2 together — traders, civil servants, retirees, port workers, everybody's estate. We are not putting this campaign on that ground. Tell us your street issue and we'll write it in the notebook.
> Then remove the incitement element and log the user. Incitement is a community-safety matter, not a debate.

## 5. Rumour-response sequence (the 15-minute protocol)

1. **Capture.** Screenshot with the URL/forward, the group name if visible, and the time. Log in the rumour register with a case number.
2. **Classify.** Is it (a) about the candidate, (b) about the process/polling, (c) about another candidate, (d) service misinformation (wrong date/unit/party)?
3. **Check the ledger.** Can it be answered with a document from `00-CLAIMS-LEDGER.md`? If yes → post T-37 with the source. If no → answer in comments only, factually, without asserting anything unheld. **(c) type: never publish a counter-rumour about a rival. Ever.**
4. **Publish once, everywhere.** One page post, then paste the link into the ward WhatsApp lists and reply it under every copy of the claim you find. Do not write five different denials; they get stitched together as "the campaign contradicts itself".
5. **Truth Room.** If the rumour will recur, add the ruling and its document to the site's Truth Room (the gate requires the evidence link) and point everything at it.
6. **Close the loop.** Note the reach, whether it died, and whether the site or record needs updating. Log dated and archived — `../ola-western-web/docs/05-OPERATIONS-RUNBOOK.md` has the matching incident playbook.

**Service misinformation** (wrong polling unit, wrong party logo, wrong date) is the most damaging class and the easiest to fix: reply in the same group within minutes with the corrected fact and the source. Post the ballot explainer (T-05) the same day.

## 6. DMs and WhatsApp — short scripts

**New joiner:**
> Welcome to the Ola Western [[WARD]] list. What you'll get: street notices, rumour checks with documents, and polling-day instructions. Nothing else. Reply REMOVE to leave at any time; your number isn't shared or sold.

**Someone reporting an impersonator:**
> Thank you. Please send the screenshot, the account name and the number used. We'll publish that it is not ours. For clarity: this campaign never asks for money, BVN, voter-card details or passwords.

**A journalist or blogger:**
> Yes — the record page has every claim with its published report and date. Who would you like to speak with, and when? [[then brief the candidate; never hand a reporter an unattested claim]]

**An angry person in DMs:**
> I hear you. What's the specific issue and which street? I'll get it in front of him with your name if you allow it.

**A rival's supporter:**
> No problem — you'll get record posts from us, and you can hold ours against yours. Ask one question of every candidate: what did you personally do, and where's the report?

## 7. Moderation table (what to do with each kind of comment)

| Kind | Action |
|---|---|
| Wrong but honest | Answer with a document. Never delete |
| Abuse, obscenity toward a person | Hide the comment, report, log |
| Ethnic/religious incitement | Hide, report, log, and consider a page-level post on tone (`03` §7) |
| Personal data (a phone number, address, PVC photo of someone else) | Hide immediately, and message the person whose data it is |
| Spam/scam ("send him a CVN to get a job") | Hide, report, and publish a warning (T-39) |
| Impersonation account | Report, screenshot, publish the official handle list, and use T-36/T-39 |
| Coordinated copy-paste attack | One factual post with the documents; do not reply line-by-line; screenshot the pattern for the log |
| Criticism of the candidate's record | Answer, thank them for asking, keep it visible, and forward the substance to the desk — that comment is free research |

## 8. Weekly comment review (15 minutes, Friday)

1. The three most-repeated hostile claims → do we have documents for all three?
2. The three best resident questions → which become next week's posts?
3. Which street issues were raised that no template covers? → send to `05`'s street captains.
4. Response time: how many first-replies missed the hour? → fix the rota, not the copy.
5. Anything that must go into the site's Truth Room with evidence attached.
