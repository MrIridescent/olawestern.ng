# 02 — Facebook post templates (42, copy-paste)

**Compiled:** 3 November 2026 · Every factual line inside these templates is drawn from `00-CLAIMS-LEDGER.md`. Anything shown as `[[SLOT]]` must be filled with a real value before posting, and anything marked **HOLD** must not be posted until the ledger releases it.
**Format:** each template gives you the use case, the paste-ready text, the first comment (where the source goes), the tags, the media spec, and the one-line reason it works.

**Global rules for all 42:** write to one person, not a crowd · one idea per post · put the point in line 1 (Facebook truncates at about three lines on mobile) · 60–140 words for a text post · end with one ask, never two · no ALL-CAPS shouts, no exclamation stacking · a photograph of a real place with a named street beats any graphic · reply to the first ten comments inside an hour (early reply velocity is the strongest organic signal you control).
