# 04 — VIDEO TEMPLATE LIBRARY (Ola Western · Amuwo-Odofin I · 2027)

Phone-shot only. One fact per clip. Shoot → caption → publish the same day. Written for one media aide and one volunteer with a phone.

Companion files: `00-CLAIMS-LEDGER.md` (the GO / HOLD / SLOT rulings behind the fact card below — check it before writing any new claim) and `01-HASHTAG-KIT.md` (tag limits per platform). If this file and the ledger ever disagree, the ledger wins and this file gets corrected.

## 1. How this works

**Three rules.** (1) One fact per clip — if it needs two facts it is two clips; never an "achievements" montage. (2) Phone only — one phone films, one phone records sound, one person holds frame and the subject's shoulder. No crew, no gear shopping, no edit longer than 40 minutes. (3) Same-day — film in the ward in the morning, cut and caption by 18:00, post 19:00–21:00. Any clip older than 5 days is re-cut, not posted late.

**Consent and no-fabrication (read aloud to whoever films).** Every identifiable person on camera who is not the candidate gives consent twice: spoken on camera ("I agree this can be posted on the Ola Western pages") and signed or thumb-printed on a slip (two per A4 page, kept with the raw footage for 5 years). Nobody is paid, given transport money, or given food to appear. We do not pull "random residents" off a waiting list — we film people already on that street, in that market, at that meeting. **We cut nothing that changes meaning:** removing pauses and "um" is fine, removing a qualifier ("not yet", "in some wards") is not. If an answer damages us we still post it, or we drop that person entirely. A claim with no attached evidence does not enter a clip in any wording, any language, at any size. No synthetic media of him: no AI voice, no AI face, no generated crowd, no "enhanced" footage of an event we did not film, no staging a crowd as spontaneous.

## 2. Format library

Universal spec: **1080×1920 9:16, 30 fps, H.264 High 8–12 Mbps, AAC 128–192 kbps**, captions in the middle 60% of height. Deviations are listed per template. Never post a re-download of a posted clip.

### T1 — One-Fact Proof Clip (20s)

It is a borehole." **Beats:** 0–3s subject at the asset, shoulder to camera, phone at chest height → 3–8s one sentence naming the fact → 8–14s three close-ups (hands on the pump, the surface, people filling) → 14–20s turn to lens: name, "APC · Amuwo-Odofin I", HQ. **Line skeleton:** "In [[MONTH/STATED YEAR]] we [[FACT-CARD VERB]] at [[STREET/LANDMARK]]. [[ONE MEASURABLE FROM THE FACT CARD]]. I am Ola Western." **B-roll:** water or wheelbarrow, road edge, a boot, a street signpost, a resident's hands — nothing downloaded. **Text/caption:** line 1 fact, line 2 date, line 3 ward; caption 2 sentences ending `[[SOURCE: outlet, date, URL]]`. **Thumb/title/desc:** his hand on the asset + "BOREHOLE · [[WARD]] · 2018" / "[[FACT]] in [[WARD]] — Ola Western" / fact + date + source + `[[HANDLE]]`. **Spec:** 9:16 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 10 Mbps, AAC 128 kbps; one locked-off take plus three close-ups, no zoom, no transitions. **Hashtags:** `#OlaWestern #FestacTown #[[WARD]]` + `[[2 MAX]]`. **Trouble:** filming an asset we cannot tie to the record, or taking credit for a road we did not build — then the street name stays a slot.

## 3. Phone production SOP

**Bag, every shoot day:** 2 phones (camera + sound/reference), clip-on lavalier with USB-C **and** Lightning adapters, power bank, small tripod, foam windscreen or a sock, lens cloth, tape, 10 consent slips, pen, printed ward map.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Framing | Tap-hold to lock AE/AF on his face before he moves. Mid-chest to two fingers above the head. Never pinch-zoom — move your feet. |
| Holding | Both hands, phone vertical, elbows pinned to ribs, heel-to-toe walk; operator walks backwards, a second person clears the path. Minimum 3 takes per shot. |
| Audio | Lav 18–25 cm from the mouth, cable taped, sock over the head. Second phone records from a chest pocket facing his mouth. Record 10s of room tone before you stop — the cut needs it. |
| Wind | Any breeze means windscreen, or film inside a doorway. If we hear wind we don't publish; we re-shoot 20s indoors. |
| Noisy market | Get to 40 cm. Ask the trader to repeat with the generator off. Shoot 07:30–09:00 or after 16:00. Unclear words become a subtitled T1, not a muddy hero clip. |
| Light | Face the light, never put it behind him. Overcast is the best studio in FESTAC. 11:30–15:00 = shade at 45°. By a window indoors; kill the ring light if the phone is fighting it. |
| Ratios | 9:16 Reels/TikTok/Shorts/WhatsApp Status. 1:1 Facebook feed. 16:9 YouTube full and town halls. Always shoot 9:16 — a square crops out of a vertical, never the reverse. |
| Captions | Burned-in, every time. Auto-captions are a first draft, never the output. Pidgin and Yoruba lines typed by hand and read back to the speaker. Max 2 lines, ~38 characters, white on a 60% black bar, middle 60% of frame. |
| File naming | `YYYYMMDD_WARD_FORMAT_TAKE##_OP.mp4` → `20261103_FESTAC2_T02_A01_T3_DAYO.mp4`. Slips scan to `..._CONSENT_01.jpg` in the same folder. |
| Off-day backup | Before leaving the location: import, then upload masters to cloud. At home/office: copy to a second drive — **two copies exist before the card is touched again**. On a rest day the aide still runs the 2-copy check and writes the clip log. |
| Raw card | **Never delete from the card.** A card is reformatted only after the editor signs the weekly log confirming two copies. Footage of a pulled-down clip still stays. |
| Export | 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264, 8–12 Mbps, AAC 128–192 kbps, one pass, upload from the phone or the editor's machine — not from a downloaded copy. |

## 4. Language: English · Pidgin · Yoruba

Publish the same clip as **three separate assets**, not three languages inside one video. Order: English (page + YouTube), Pidgin (Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok — widest reach), Yoruba (community forwards, Festac III and older voters). Re-export the burned captions and the CTA end card in the target language so it never reads as a machine dub; the picture may stay identical, but we never re-dub his recorded voice.

- He speaks in the language the room uses. The other two versions are captioned, not re-voiced.
- **Yoruba is not approved by the writer.** Every Yoruba title, caption and burned line is read aloud by a native-speaking team member who confirms (a) tone marks and spelling, (b) that it doesn't sound translated, (c) that it hasn't become a proverb carrying a different meaning. Log the reviewer's initials and time before the asset goes out. No unreviewed Yoruba asset, ever.
- Pidgin house style, one sheet for the whole slate: "na", "abi", "dem", "wan do", "e be like say".
- **Example Pidgin lines — examples only, for the campaign to adapt, and the facts inside them still have to come from the fact card:** "Na so water dey pass for this drain when rain fall — make we look am properly." / "I no come with promise, I come with the paper wey show wetin dem do." / "If una road spoil, take photo, carry am come our office for 3rd Avenue."
- No religious or ethnic framing in any version, including Yoruba greetings that imply a faith. HOLD applies: nothing about living or worshipping inside FESTAC Town, in any language.

## 7. Refusals — what this team does not do with video

| We refuse | Why it protects the campaign |
|---|---|
| Synthetic/AI video or audio of him — voice clone, face swap, generated crowd, AI "b-roll" of FESTAC, upscaled fake footage | One exposed synthetic clip cancels 21 press sources; his entire argument is that he is verifiable. |
| Paying, feeding or transporting anyone to appear as an unprompted "random resident" | Residents talk. A paid extra becomes the story, and every genuine testimonial after it is presumed bought. |
| Staging a crowd, a queue or "surprise" support and filming it as spontaneous | Turnout is the most fact-checked claim in any campaign; a full room is measurable from any window, and platforms strike deceptive content. |
| Cutting a resident's words into a meaning they didn't say, or chopping a question so the answer lands better | We lose one clip; they lose their voice. "Consent + we cut nothing that changes meaning" is the only reason people keep agreeing to be filmed. |
| Re-broadcasting enemy-fabricated rumours, including in our own headline while "clarifying" | Repetition is distribution; T4 answers an accusation once, in its exact words, against a document, and never re-serves it weekly. |
| Covert filming of people who did not consent — homes, worshippers' gatherings, opponents' private meetings, children | One illegal recording hands rivals and the state a case against the media unit, and one seized phone ends the slate. |
| Claiming credit for assets we cannot evidence, "six roads built" when the record says approved, or sponsorship of Penalty King / Ya Koo Yo | Overstatement is the cheapest thing for a journalist to disprove and the most expensive to retract: approved is not built, attended is not sponsored. |
| The four HOLD items until evidence is attached | No evidence link exists yet; the day one is dated and attached, the clip can be built — until then, no wording, no caption, no "everybody knows". |

**Escalation:** if a compliance doubt survives 10 minutes of discussion, the clip does not go out today. Log `HOLD [[reason]]`, shoot two more T1s instead, and the slate does not slip.
