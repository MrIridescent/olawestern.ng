## 2. The record — eight evidenced lines (this is the campaign's whole case)

Each line below has a published report behind it. Copy the **post-ready sentence** and paste the **source** in the first comment or as the link.

**R-1 · Water — six wards (March 2018) · GO**
Post-ready: *"In March 2018, Ola Western financed and built clean-water borehole facilities across six wards of Amuwo-Odofin, working with local government stakeholders."*
Source: FestacOnline, "Good news: 6 wards to get boreholes in Amuwo-Odofin" — `festaconline.com.ng/good-news-6-wardsto-get-boreholes-amuwoodofin/`
Also usable: The Nation, "Council partners investor on CSR projects in Amuwo-Odofin" (March 2018).
Don't say: "he solved water in FESTAC" (the record is six wards and a continuing demand for pipe-borne water). Don't say "11 wards" — that figure is not sourced.

**R-2 · Drainage — 7th Avenue (March 2018) · GO**
Post-ready: *"After residents and the legislative council raised the blocked arterial drainage on 7th Avenue, FESTAC, Ola Western moved heavy earth-moving equipment in and got the desilting done in March 2018 — extending earlier clearing work in Mile 2 Wards A1 and A2."*
Source: FestacOnline, "7th Avenue, Festac to get support on drainage clearing" — `festaconline.com.ng/7th-avenue-festac-to-get-support-on-drainage-clearing/`

## 5. Plans and planks (forward-looking content is not a claim about the past — label it)

Cleared from the ARBR pledge, the profile document's platform statements and the intake strategy papers. Always in future tense, always "if elected".

1. **Permanent constituent consultation** — a published, recurring ward-level accountability session rather than a five-year visit. *(ARBR pledge, attested.)*
2. **State capital projects for FESTAC** — continuing the road-approval work into funded, completed phases. *(ARBR pledge + R-4.)*
3. **Technical skills for youth** — construction trades, clean-energy installation, digital skills and logistics, positioned as an alternative to street-gang involvement. *(profile document, platform statement.)*
4. **Market and SME protection** — predictable enforcement, protection against arbitrary levies, market security and access to credit. *(strategy documents; LAWMA street-trading enforcement needs "balanced messaging".)*
5. **Mile 2 mobility** — traffic management, trailer-bridge parking, and basic municipal service delivery on the Mile 2 axis. *(strategy ward table.)*
6. **Canal, drainage and FHA matters** — canal maintenance, ground-rent and estate-oversight disputes that bear on Festac III. *(strategy ward table; 23 Road remains a standing request, status `under_review`.)*
7. **Water as a continuing demand** — pipe-borne water remains an estate-forum ask; R-1 is boreholes, not a solved problem. *(digital strategy.)*

**23 Road:** the campaign's own issue tracker lists rehabilitation as `under_review` with a standing request, while press reports credit the LGA chairman with flagging off Road 23 works and a "after 20 years, Festac roads get facelift" story. Say *"a standing request we are pushing"*, never *"we got 23 Road done"*. That distinction is where campaigns get caught.
