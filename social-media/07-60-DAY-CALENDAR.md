# 07 — The 60-day calendar (and the 15 days after it)

**Compiled:** 3 November 2026
**Anchoring arithmetic, stated so nobody has to guess:** Day 1 = **Tuesday 3 November 2026**. Day 60 = **Friday 1 January 2027**. The presidential and National Assembly poll on INEC's published timetable is **Saturday 16 January 2027** = **Day 75**, which is 74 days from today. Gubernatorial election, 6 February 2027 = Day 96.

So the honest read of "we have precisely 60 days": the 60-day theatre programme runs **3 November – 1 January**, and then there are **14 more days** of get-out-the-vote before the ballot. Both blocks are in here, because stopping on Day 60 would waste the whole plan. Recompute these day numbers every Monday; a calendar that quietly slips by three days is how campaigns arrive at the polling unit late.

Columns: **FB** = template from `02`, **VID** = format from `04`, **STREET** = action from `05`, **METRIC** = what you check on Friday (`06` §7).

## Day 75 · Saturday 16 January 2027 — poll day
- 05:30 — agents confirm presence at every unit; failures escalated immediately (`05` §8).
- 06:30 — page post: T-41 (open, service, no advocacy at the unit) **[CONFIRM election-day campaigning rules first]**.
- Through the day — a post every ~3 hours: turnout observations from your own agents only, no national speculation, no unverified numbers.
- Any incident: photo + unit name + time to the war-room WhatsApp; the runbook playbook for polling-day outage applies.
- **No result claims before the party and INEC process.** A campaign that announces its own victory from Facebook at 8pm and loses on the sheet has destroyed its credibility for the next cycle.
