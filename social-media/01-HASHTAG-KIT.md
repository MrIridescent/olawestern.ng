# 01 — Hashtag kit

**Compiled:** 3 November 2026 · Every tag below was chosen for a Lagos State House of Assembly race in Amuwo-Odofin Constituency I, not for generic reach. Verify the current volume and spelling of any tag in Facebook's own search before relying on it — hashtag popularity shifts within days in Nigerian political traffic, and a tag that has been hijacked by an opponent's content becomes a liability.

---

## 1. The five rules that actually move reach on Facebook

1. **Three to five tags, never thirty.** Facebook's own guidance and behaviour is that over-tagging looks like spam and suppresses distribution. Instagram tolerates more (up to ~10 used well); TikTok wants 3–5 that describe the content plus one or two community tags. Put the surplus in the first comment if you must have them.
2. **One identity tag, one place tag, one issue tag, one action tag.** That is the whole formula. Example: `#OlaWestern` + `#FestacTown` + `#Drainage` + `#2027`.
3. **The place tag is the algorithm's only clue that this matters to someone in Amuwo-Odofin.** Never drop it. Reach in a state-house race is worthless unless it is local.
4. **Never use a tag you can't verify you own.** `#OlaWestern` is the campaign's identity tag; check whether an opponent or an impersonator is running content under lookalikes (`#OlaWestern2027`, `#OlaWesternNG`) and, if so, publish the difference from `06` §1 rather than fighting in tags.
5. **Tags are not claims.** No `#Verified`, `#INEC`, or any tag that implies institutional endorsement the campaign does not have. No tag that references another candidate's identity tag in order to siphon their traffic — that is platform-policy bait and it reads as pettiness to the neighbours who matter.

## 3. Tags to avoid, and why

| Avoid | Reason |
|---|---|
| `#APCRules`-style national party tags as your main anchor | You will compete with every APC page in Nigeria and lose. The seat is local; the tags should be too. Use the party name in the body |
| `#Tinubu`, `#SanwoOlu`, `#PeterObi`, `#Atiku` and any candidate tag you do not own | Borrowed-traffic tagging; invites the wrong comment section, and in the president/governor's case it drags a state-house post into national arguments that lose you neighbours |
| `#Breaking`, `#Trending`, `#Viral` | Zero local meaning, spam signal, and it dates the campaign badly when the thing isn't |
| `#ElectionRigging`, `#INECFraud` before any attested incident | Preemptive delegitimisation. It is unlawful speech territory, it is exactly the "fear content" the refusals list excludes, and it gets a page flagged |
| `#VoteBuyersExposed`, `#CashForVotes` as campaign tags | Accusatory hashtags without a named, evidenced incident expose the campaign to defamation claims and provoke the one thing that hurts most on the street — traders and agents turning on your team |
| Ethnic or religious tags (`#Yoruba`, `#Muslim`, `#Christian`, `#Igbira`, any ethno-religious marker) in a state-house race | Amuwo-Odofin's Mile 2 is deliberately multi-ethnic. Ethno-religious tagging is the fastest way to convert a service campaign into a community conflict. Faith *institutions* appear in the record because they did real work — that is different from using identity as a targeting tag |
| `#OlaWestern` misspellings and look-alikes (`#OlaWesten`, `#OlaWestren`, `#OlaWestern1`) | They split your own tag's traffic and help impersonators rank above you |
| 2023-era tags from the last cycle that the opponent now owns | Search first. If a tag is dominated by 2023 argument, skip it |

## 4. The 60-day rotation

Do not run the same five tags for two months; platforms and people both tune out. Rotate the place/issue slot while holding identity constant:

| Weeks | Fixed | Rotate in |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 (3–16 Nov) | `#OlaWestern #TowardsResponsibleRepresentation` | `#FestacTown`, `#AmuwoOdofin`, `#HisRecordIsOpen` |
| 3–4 (17–30 Nov) | same | ward tags in rotation: `#FestacI`, `#23Road`, `#7thAvenue`, `#Mile2` |
| 5–6 (1–14 Dec) | same | issue tags: `#FestacRoads`, `#CleanWater`, `#Drainage`, `#YouthSkills` |
| 7–8 (15–28 Dec) | same | `#Accountability`, `#DeliveringBeforeOffice`, `#FactNotRumour` |
| 9–10 (29 Dec – 11 Jan) | `#OlaWestern #AmuwoOdofinConstituencyI` | `#16January2027`, `#VoteOlaWestern`, service tags on the day |

## 5. First-comment discipline

Facebook rewards dwell time and punishes link-stuffed posts. So:
- Post the copy with 3–5 tags.
- Put the source link in the **first comment**, as the campaign, within two minutes: *"Source: The Sun, March 2024 — over 1,000 persons benefit from Ola Western Food Festival in Festac. [link]"*. Then pin that comment.
- That first comment is also where a `block`-grade claim becomes a defensible one. A claim with the document one click away is the whole strategy in one habit.
