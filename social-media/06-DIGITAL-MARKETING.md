# 06 — Digital marketing (page, cadence, paid, WhatsApp, metrics)

**Compiled:** 3 November 2026
Scope: everything that happens on a screen in this constituency, except the video formats (`04`) and the physical street game (`05`). This is the operating manual for the Facebook page and the messaging layer around it.

## 2. Cadence and timing

Measured behaviour in this market: Facebook carries the constituency, WhatsApp carries the decision, and the phone is data-limited. So:

- **Two posts a day minimum** on the page, one video per day, one story per day. Never more than three posts a day — the fourth gets nothing and trains followers to ignore you.
- **Slots:** 07:30–08:30 (before work), 13:00–14:00 (market/lunch), 20:00–21:30 (the golden hour — phones, data on home wifi). Run the record posts at 20:00 and the service notices at 07:30.
- **Sunday** is family and community tone, not advocacy.
- **Batch:** write four posts on Friday for the following week; leave one slot free for whatever happens on the street that day. Reactive beats planned, but planned keeps the page alive when nothing happens.
- **Data discipline:** images under ~300 KB, video under ~60 MB, always captioned burned-in, never 4K for a 5G-less audience. A post that costs a subscriber 20MB is a post that gets scrolled.

## 3. The distribution loop (the "theatre" part)

Every single street or field activity runs the same five-step cycle. This is the tactic that makes a small team look like a movement — honestly, because it's real activity, photographed same-day.

1. **Announce** (T-29) the night before — place, time, no hype.
2. **Do** the activity. One media pair is assigned to every activity; no activity happens unfilmed.
3. **Post same evening**: one video (`04` T2/T3), one photo carousel, one notebook-page photo (T-30). Timeliness is worth more than polish — a same-night phone clip outranks a next-week edited one.
4. **Seed** into the ward WhatsApp lists with the caption, not a link (links die in preview).
5. **Harvest** the comments into the issue log and the rumour register (`09` §5, §8), then turn the two best questions into next week's posts.

## 4. Paid reach — what to buy, and the rules first

**Rules to confirm before spending one naira** (`08` §5 has the checklist): Meta's political/social-issue advertising requirements apply in Nigeria — identity verification and disclosure labelling for such ads **[CONFIRM the current flow, required ID and whether "Paid for by" disclosure is enforced for Nigerian state races as at this date]**; INEC's rules on campaigning periods and broadcast **[CONFIRM]**; and the Electoral Act's campaign-financing limits and record-keeping, reported at ₦10bn for presidential candidates in 2026 with weak enforcement **[unverified as to current gazetted figures]**. Keep every receipt and a running spend log from Day 1 — this is the paperwork that becomes a problem in February, not November.

**The allocation the campaign's own strategy documents set: 50 / 30 / 20.**

| Bucket | Share | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness (50%)** | Reach and frequency inside the constituency only | Photo/record posts boosted to a location polygon covering Festac + Mile 2, age 18+, no interest targeting (interest targeting shrinks a local race and wastes money) |
| **Conversion/ask (30%)** | WhatsApp-click and event-RSVP objectives | T-34 (join the list), T-33 (volunteer), T-29 (event notice) — the posts that produce an identifier you can contact later |
| **Defence/rapid (20%)** | Held back, unspent | Rumour-response boosting (T-37) and polling-eve service messages. Do not commit this at the start; it is the reserve that wins November |

**Geography discipline is a hard rule from the campaign's own documents:** targeting restricted strictly to Constituency I wards. Boosting to all of Lagos buys applause from people who cannot vote in this seat and burns the reserve.

**Creative rules for paid:** never run a HOLD claim in an ad; never run an ad that a neighbour would find embarrassing in the comment section; put the source link in the first comment of every boosted post; refresh creative every 7–10 days or frequency fatigue eats the budget.

## 6. Comment and community operations

- Two people rostered per shift, 07:00–22:00, one of them named on the page as the responder.
- `09-REPLY-KIT.md` is the only source of approved replies; improvisation on hostile claims is how a campaign creates its own second story.
- **Never** argue with a resident about politics in a comment; answer the specific question and stop.
- Escalation: anything alleging a crime, naming a real person accusingly, or claiming the campaign did something it didn't goes to the campaign secretary within 15 minutes, before a reply.
- Every post's first ten comments get replies within the hour — that is the strongest organic lever available and it costs nothing.

## 7. Metrics — the weekly review sheet (Friday, 20 minutes)

| Metric | How | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Reach in-constituency | Page insights, location breakdown | Rising with no paid spend; ≥ 60% of reach inside the four wards |
| **Ward comments** | Manual count of comments from named streets/wards | This is the real number. If streets stop commenting, the copy is drifting off-constituency |
| **Shares per post** | Insights | Median share rate is the format test; kill a format after three attempts below median (`03` §8) |
| WhatsApp joiners | Count of "JOIN [[WARD]]" messages | 1 net new joiner per 100 reached is a healthy ask post |
| Volunteers signed | Tally sheet | Any number above zero; the ward distribution matters more than the total |
| Video completion | Platform metric | ≥ 40% at 15s on a 20s clip; below that, the hook is wrong |
| Rumour cases logged & closed | Register | Time-to-first-public-answer < 15 minutes on the emerging ones |
| Site visits from social | Site analytics | Only meaningful if the site is deployed — **[gap]** no production host is assigned yet, so publish the URL only after `docs/12` Phase 2 clears |
| Spend vs cap | Receipt log | 100% of spend recorded against the bucket plan, weekly |

**Do not chase:** follower count, likes, or virality outside the constituency. None of them vote in Amuwo-Odofin I.

## 8. What not to do (operational, not moral — these simply lose races)

Buying followers (kills reach forever, and the drop is visible in insights); engagement bait ("tag 3 friends", "comment YES if you agree") which platforms downgrade; auto-DM blasts from a page; running a claim that isn't in the ledger; posting a photo of a supporter's face or a PVC without consent; deleting criticism; changing handles mid-race; scheduling a week of posts without a human watching comments; boosting a post that has hostile comments at the top (fix the thread first or the ad pays to show strangers the argument); letting five different volunteers run five different versions of the page voice.
