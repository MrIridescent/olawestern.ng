1. **Who:** "Hon. Engr. Folorunsho Olaitan Segun — Ola Western. A civil engineer who runs Western Concrete here in FESTAC Town, and the APC candidate for Lagos State House of Assembly, Amuwo-Odofin Constituency I."
2. **What he did:** "Boreholes in six wards in March 2018. The 7th Avenue drainage desilted the same month. A written technical case to the Governor's office that won approval for six FESTAC roads, headlined by 1st Avenue. Over 1,000 households fed in March 2024. Mile 2 youths written into the NIWA jetty project in 2025."
3. **Why him:** "Every line has a published report behind it — over twenty of them. Ask any other candidate for the same list, with dates."
4. **The mandate:** "He won the APC primary in 2022 with 12 of 20 delegates, and in May 2026 the party made him its consensus flagbearer with over 3,000 votes."
5. **The promise:** "Permanent constituent consultation, capital projects for FESTAC, technical skills for youth — pledged publicly at the ARBR forum in August 2026, and we will publish four things every quarter whether they flatter us or not."

## 2. Do not say these four things (HOLD — no document yet)

- A hospital-bills relief fund
- That he sponsored the Penalty King tournaments
- That he funds vigilante patrols
- That he lives and worships inside FESTAC Town

Say instead: *"we're documenting it — ask us for the file"*, or the plan form: *"what he will push for is…"*. The website's own audit currently blocks all four lines; a Facebook post that asserts what the site refuses to publish is the hole the campaign gets judged on.

**Also never:** claim he *built* the roads (he secured the approval — government executes); add "11 wards engaged" or "100% consensus"; name an individual as endorsing without a photo, document or their written words; assert anything about the 2023 result detail or a rival's defection; claim a council title he has not held.

## 4. Escalate immediately (before answering) — five cases

1. Anything alleging a crime, naming a person accusingly, or claiming we did something we didn't → campaign secretary.
2. A resident's personal data appearing in a comment (phone, address, PVC photo) → hide, message the person, log.
3. Ethnic or religious incitement → hide, report, log, and warn the captains in that ward.
4. An impersonation page or a money request in our name → screenshot, T-39 warning, report to the platform.
5. Anything about a HOLD line, a rival's health/family/character, or a "result" → nobody answers. Secretary only.
