**Compiled:** 3 November 2026 · Every live-status claim was checked on this date against the source named in the row. Items marked **[confirm]** could not be verified as current and must be re-checked before you spend an hour on them; items marked **[closed?]** were found as dated evidence of a *category*, not as an open opportunity.

The positioning first, because it decides which half of this file is worth your time.

## 5. Nigeria-specific routes for the 2027 cycle (hat C — and the largest near-term market)

**The arithmetic that makes this the priority:** INEC's published timetable places the presidential and National Assembly election at **16 January 2027** and gubernatorial elections at **6 February 2027**. At compilation that is roughly **74 days** to the presidential poll and about **99 days** to the gubernatorial poll — recompute before quoting. Every candidate who will spend money on a website spends it between now and December, and the 2027 state assemblies, Senate and House of Representatives races mean **thousands of seats**, each with a candidate, a committee and a budget.

| Channel | How to work it |
|---|---|
| **Constituency-level campaign committees, 2027 cycle** | Direct, via `PITCH-KIT.md` §2. Target state assembly and federal seats before governors' offices — smaller principals buy faster and refer better. Aim for **one named introduction per day** from your existing network |
| **Political party secretariats (state and national)** | Sell the multi-seat package (`PITCH-KIT.md` §7, Model 2): one platform, many candidates, shared evidence discipline. The buyer is the state organising secretary or the media/directorates, not the candidate |
| **Aspirant consultations** | A paid diagnostic — "here is your digital state, here is what leaks, here is what you need before the primary" — is a low-commitment entry product and a filter for serious buyers. Price it as a fixed, small, real deliverable |
| **PR, communications and campaign-consultancy firms in Lagos/Abuja** | They sell campaigns and cannot build software. Be the technical subcontractor. Approach the account director, not the founder |
| **Media houses and political programmes** | Fact-checking desks and election-night data needs overlap with the Truth Room pattern; a licensing or building conversation |
| **INEE/ISEC? no — civil society election-observation ecosystem** | YIAGA Africa, the Nigeria Network of NGOs, CDD West Africa, Transition Solutions Group, the Macarthur/FFI-funded observation coalitions — they need results-reporting, evidence-verification and parallel-vote-tabulation tooling and rarely have an engineer on staff |
| **Professional bodies for the comms half** | NIPR (Nigerian Institute of Public Relations) and ANRP membership gives you the vocabulary and the room where the principals sit. Note: their codes of practice are also why everything in this pack stays anonymised without client consent (`README.md` §1) |
| **Tech-community credibility in-country** | Lagos Dev meetups, Google Developer Groups, PYCON/Nigeria tech weeks, ALX/Andela alumni networks, Yaba-cluster founder circles — the referral surface for both hats. Offer to give a talk on "the publish gate": a talk converts better than a pitch |
| **Government and parastatal digital work** | State ICT agencies and ministries publish contracts; the integrity/documentation posture is a differentiator in a category with a credibility deficit | **[confirm]** current tender windows |

**The refusal that will cost you a fee, and to make anyway:** requests for bulk WhatsApp blasting, fake engagement, paid commentators, AI audio/video of a real person, or a Truth Room ruling about an opponent without proof. Say no in one breath and offer the alternative (`PITCH-KIT.md` §8). In a market this small, the no is the brand.

## 6. Making the work visible (the channel that compounds)

Ranked by expected value per hour spent:

1. **Strip the pattern out and open-source it.** Nothing in the Live Desk contract, the publish-gate rule, the integrity formula or the image-header probe is client-specific. Extract them into a clean, MIT-licensed TypeScript package with no data, no names, no media — `campaign-record-Kit` or similar — with the case study as the README. That single artefact does more for a hiring manager's opinion of you than any portfolio page, and it is the only honest way to show code from this project (`public/` holds 83 third-party press photographs and `data/db.json` holds real supporters' details — **neither leaves the machine**). **[do this only after confirming you hold the rights to publish that code — check the contract with the campaign]**
2. **Publish the infographic.** `docs/infographic.html` as a PDF and as a hosted page, with the anonymisation intact. It is the artefact a stranger can read in five minutes and remember.
3. **Write three specific posts**, not a blog: (a) *The publish gate: enforcing editorial integrity in the server, not the style guide*; (b) *The public page is the CMS — building Live Desk from a 25-field contract*; (c) *Why my campaign site publishes a trust score of 40 and refuses to be shared*. Post to dev.to / Hacker News ("Show HN" for the open-source package) / LinkedIn. Title (c) is the one that travels.
4. **A one-page portfolio site** with three case entries, measured numbers dated, and a stated limits section. Keep it under 100 kB — you can defend that in an interview about a performance-minded engineer.
5. **A talk.** Offer it to YIAGA's sandbox cohort, Code for Africa, GDG Lagos, any civic-tech meetup. Speakers get the referrals that applicants don't.

## 8. Application / outreach log format

Keep it as a plain text or CSV file next to this folder and update it weekly. Political-technology procurement runs on being remembered.

```
DATE | CHANNEL | SECTION REF | CONTACT (name, org, how you got them) | HAT (A/B/C) |
SENT (letter variant) | OPENS/DEADLINE | RESPONSE | NEXT ACTION + DATE | OUTCOME
```

Three fields matter most: **how you got them** (referral source), **next action with a date**, and **outcome**. Review the log every Friday and count touches, not replies — replies lag touches by weeks in this sector.
